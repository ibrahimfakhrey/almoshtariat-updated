Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I get started with ALMOSHTRIYAT?

Simply click "Get Started" to create your account. Choose between buyer or supplier registration, complete your company profile, and start connecting with verified partners.

Is there a setup fee?

No, registration and basic platform access are completely free. You only pay transaction fees when you successfully complete orders through our platform.

How are suppliers verified?

All suppliers go through a comprehensive verification process including document validation, business license checks, and quality assessments before joining our platform.

What payment methods do you accept?

We support multiple payment methods including bank transfers, credit cards, and digital wallets. All transactions are secured with end-to-end encryption.

Do you offer customer support?

Yes, we provide 24/7 customer support through chat, email, and phone. Our dedicated team is always ready to help you with any questions or issues.

Can I integrate with my existing systems?

Absolutely! We offer API integration and can work with your existing ERP, accounting, and inventory management systems for seamless operations.
