About ALMOSHTRIYAT

Revolutionizing B2B procurement in Egypt and the Middle East through innovative technology and smart solutions.

Our Story

Founded in 2023, ALMOSHTRIYAT emerged from a simple observation: B2B procurement in Egypt was fragmented, inefficient, and lacked transparency. Traditional procurement processes were time-consuming, prone to errors, and often resulted in suboptimal supplier relationships.


Our founders, with decades of combined experience in technology and supply chain management, envisioned a platform that would bridge the gap between buyers and suppliers, creating a seamless, transparent, and efficient procurement ecosystem.


Today, ALMOSHTRIYAT serves thousands of companies across Egypt, from small businesses to large enterprises, helping them streamline their procurement processes and build stronger supplier relationships.
